Smart Wave Solar · Meta performance reviewPlan · weeks 2 to 4 · 10 / 13

Weeks 2 to 4: teach Meta who buys, then scale

Meta finds more of whatever we tell it is a win. Right now that is any lead. The next step is telling it which leads book and buy.

Signal

Send lead outcomes back to Meta

Update lead stages in Leads Center, or connect NetSuite through the Conversions API, so qualified, booked and sold leads flow back each week.

Once enough outcomes arrive, form ad sets can optimise for leads that convert, not just leads.

Audiences

Customer list for exclusions and as a seed

Stop showing ads to existing customers, and give Meta's Advantage+ audience a picture of who buys from you.

Cuts wasted impressions and points delivery at lookalike homeowners.

Retargeting

Retarget page visitors, form openers and video viewers

A small ad set for people who visited the page, opened a form without submitting, or watched half a video in the last 30 days, launched once each audience passes about 1,000 people.

Warm audiences typically cost less per lead than cold ones.

Page

Speed up the landing pages

27% of Utah clicks drop before the page loads. Compressing images and trimming scripts on the Leadpages build should recover part of that.

More page views from the same spend is the cheapest volume there is.

Structure

Consolidate for learning, then scale budgets

Each ad set needs about 50 leads a week to settle. At $800 a day we make about 77 a week across three ad sets. After the Colorado test, winners move into fewer, larger ad sets before budgets rise.

Fewer ad sets with more data per ad set give steadier cost per lead at scale.

Offer

Launch Version B pages for bigger systems

The pages showing larger systems and batteries are built and waiting on your "from" prices.

Raises the value of each lead without raising its cost.
