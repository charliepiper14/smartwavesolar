5Before we spend a dollar

Four things to fix first

1

Tracking isn't connected

No dataset is linked to the ad account. The main site fires PageView only, and the survey lander has no pixel at all. Meta can't learn from what it can't see.

2

The best ad's claim has expired

The winning copy promised a 30% federal tax credit through 2032. The residential clean energy credit ended for spending after 31 December 2025, so that message can't run again.

3

The lander undercuts your story

Its footer describes a free service linking homeowners to independent contractors, with actors in the ads. That's the opposite of local, no pressure, our own team.

4

No audiences exist

No custom audiences, lookalikes or exclusions. We start from zero, which also means a clean build with nothing old to undo.

Checked 25 September 2026 with Meta Pixel Helper, Events Manager and Ads Manager. Tax credit status per IRS guidance on the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.

Ask who owns pixel 1687987861460382 and who PB is. The tax credit change hits every competitor too, so the market resets to savings, backup power and trust. That plays to Smart Wave's strengths.
