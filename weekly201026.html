Weeks 2 to 4: teach Meta who buys, then scale

Once the lead tag only counts real page leads, the next step is telling Meta which of those leads book and buy.

Signal

Send lead outcomes back to Meta

Lead stages in Leads Center for form leads, and booked or sold outcomes for website leads, flowing back each week.

6 Qualified and 0 Converted so far. Outcomes are what let form ad sets optimise for leads that buy.

Audiences

Customer list for exclusions and as a seed

Stop showing ads to existing customers, and give Meta a picture of who buys from you.

Cuts wasted impressions and points delivery at the right homeowners.

Retargeting

Retarget page visitors and form openers

Audiences go in this week. A small ad set launches once each audience is large enough to deliver well.

Warm audiences usually cost less per lead than cold ones.

Page

Speed up the landing pages

About 1 in 6 Utah clicks and 1 in 5 Colorado clicks drop before the page loads.

More page views from the same spend is the cheapest volume there is.

Structure

Consolidate for learning, then scale

Each ad set needs about 50 leads a week to settle. Winners move into fewer, larger ad sets before budgets rise.

More data per ad set gives a steadier cost per lead at scale.

Offer

Launch Version B pages for bigger systems

Built and waiting on your "from" prices.

Raises the value of each lead without raising its cost.
